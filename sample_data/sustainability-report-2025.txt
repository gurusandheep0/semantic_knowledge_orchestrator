NORTHSTAR MANUFACTURING — SUSTAINABILITY REPORT 2025

Climate commitments
Northstar has committed to reducing absolute Scope 1 and Scope 2 greenhouse-gas emissions by 42 percent from the 2022 baseline by December 2030. The organization will reach this target through renewable electricity contracts, high-efficiency production equipment, and the electrification of its regional vehicle fleet. A separate Scope 3 supplier-engagement program aims to have 70 percent of suppliers by emissions coverage publish science-aligned targets by 2028.

Current performance
During 2025, operational emissions fell 18 percent against the 2022 baseline. Renewable sources supplied 64 percent of electricity across owned facilities, up from 47 percent in 2024. The Pune facility completed a rooftop solar installation expected to generate 1.8 gigawatt-hours annually. Water withdrawal per unit of production declined 11 percent year over year.

Governance and reporting
The Sustainability Steering Council reviews progress every quarter. The Chief Operations Officer owns the climate transition plan, while site leaders maintain facility-level action registers. Material environmental metrics receive limited external assurance before publication. Any variance greater than five percentage points from the annual target requires a corrective action plan within 30 days.

Circularity
Northstar diverted 91 percent of production waste from landfill in 2025. The 2027 goal is 95 percent diversion and a 30 percent reduction in virgin plastic packaging. Product teams must complete a lifecycle review before a new physical product reaches design approval.
