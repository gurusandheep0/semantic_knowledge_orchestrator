LUMEN WORKS — PEOPLE OPERATIONS GUIDE

Flexible work
Employees may work remotely up to three days each week with manager approval. Teams publish two shared collaboration days per quarter. Employees working from another country require advance approval from People Operations and Tax because permanent-establishment and payroll obligations may apply.

Time off
Full-time employees receive 18 days of annual paid time off during their first two years, 22 days from years three through five, and 26 days after five completed years. Unused time off up to five days carries into the next calendar year. Sick leave and statutory leave are administered separately.

Learning allowance
Each employee receives an annual professional development allowance of 1,200 dollars. Eligible expenses include role-relevant courses, certifications, conferences, and technical books. Manager approval is required before purchase, and reimbursement claims must be filed within 30 days.

Performance conversations
Managers hold quarterly growth conversations covering impact, capability development, workload, and next-quarter priorities. The annual review consolidates these conversations and includes a calibration process designed to improve consistency across teams.
