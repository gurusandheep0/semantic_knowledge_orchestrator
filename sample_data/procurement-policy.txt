ORBITAL LABS — PROCUREMENT AND VENDOR POLICY

Purchase approvals
Department managers may approve standard purchases up to 10,000 dollars. Purchases from 10,001 to 25,000 dollars require director approval. Vendor purchases over 25,000 dollars require approval from both the business-unit vice president and Finance. Any purchase over 100,000 dollars also requires review by Strategic Sourcing.

Vendor onboarding
New vendors must complete sanctions screening, tax validation, information-security review, and conflict-of-interest disclosure before receiving a purchase order. Vendors that process confidential or personal data require a signed data-processing agreement. Procurement maintains the authoritative vendor record.

Competitive bids and exceptions
At least three competitive quotes are required for purchases over 50,000 dollars unless a documented sole-source exception is approved by Procurement. Emergency purchases may proceed with verbal executive authorization, but the supporting record must be completed within two business days.

Payment terms
The standard payment term is net 45 days from receipt of a valid invoice. Early-payment discounts may be accepted when the effective annual return exceeds the corporate treasury threshold. Purchase splitting to avoid an approval threshold is prohibited.
